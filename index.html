About the Game

Exbots Revolution is a free, action-packed 3D shooter featuring platformer movement controls. Dive into high-octane sci-fi combat, battle through enemy robot waves, navigate intense level layouts, and upgrade your arsenal to liberate the battlefield.

Key Features

  • 3D Sci-Fi Combat: Stunning 3D visuals paired with responsive platforming shooter controls.
  • Challenging Enemy Forces: Fight against diverse mechanical enemies and tough combat encounters.
  • Weaponry & Upgrades: Unlock powerful guns and tactical gear to boost your combat effectiveness.
  • Action-Packed Levels: Traverse dynamic stages designed for fast-paced gunplay and mobility.
